# Code of conduct

[← Back to Pineria](README.md)

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project about other people's file archives, and the issues
here often contain someone's frustration at the end of a long day. Assume the person on the other
side is doing their best with the information they had.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## What that looks like here

Behaviour that helps:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people using Pineria, not just for ourselves

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission — **including file paths and screenshots that expose someone's personal
  files**, which is a real risk in a project like this one
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, release comments —
and whenever someone is representing the project in a public space.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainer, [@EnemyOfTheScorpion](https://github.com/EnemyOfTheScorpion). If you
would rather not raise something in public, say so in a discussion or an issue without detail and a
private channel will be arranged; do not use the security advisory form for conduct reports, which
is for problems that risk people's files.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security
of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.

### Consequences

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining what was inappropriate and why, possibly
   with a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the
   people involved for a stated period.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with
   the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project.
